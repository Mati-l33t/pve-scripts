## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Find the generated BIFROST_ADMIN_PASSWORD in /opt/bifrost_data/.env inside the container. Add provider credentials and create virtual keys in the dashboard.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Use an HTTPS reverse proxy for access beyond a trusted network. Authentication is enabled for both the dashboard and inference API.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Keep a backup of /opt/bifrost_data, including .env. Losing or changing BIFROST_ENCRYPTION_KEY makes stored provider credentials unreadable.</div>
</div>
