## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/bytechef.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>There is no default account: the first sign-up (Create account on the login page) becomes the administrator and closes sign-up. Anyone who reaches the port can claim it, so do it right away.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Webhook and OAuth2 URLs come from BYTECHEF_PUBLIC_URL in /opt/bytechef_data/.env. Behind a reverse proxy or domain, change it and run systemctl restart bytechef.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>This build runs the Enterprise Edition. Its enterprise modules (embedded iPaaS, API platform, AI gateway, SSO, audit log) are free for development and testing; production use needs a subscription.</div>
</div>
