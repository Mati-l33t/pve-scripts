## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/bulwark.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Without a preset JMAP URL, finish the setup wizard with the token from <code>journalctl -u bulwark</code>. It expires after an hour; <code>systemctl restart bulwark</code> issues a new one.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Needs Stalwart 0.16.6 or newer over HTTPS with a certificate both the browser and Bulwark trust, and Permissive CORS enabled. A private CA goes into NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS in /opt/bulwark_data/.env.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>COOKIE_SECURE=false in /opt/bulwark_data/.env lets sign-in work over plain HTTP; remove it once Bulwark is served through HTTPS.</div>
</div>
