## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVED/main/ct/cookcli.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Recipes are stored in /opt/cookcli/recipes. Sample recipes are added on first install.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Add your own .cook recipe files to /opt/cookcli/recipes and restart the service.</div>
</div>
