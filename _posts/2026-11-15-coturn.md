## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Point your app (e.g. Synapse turn_shared_secret) at the static-auth-secret in /opt/coturn_data/.env. Relaying to private IP ranges is denied; add allowed-peer-ip in /etc/turnserver.conf for internal media servers.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Behind NAT, set external-ip in /etc/turnserver.conf and forward 3478 (UDP/TCP), 5349 (TCP) and UDP 49160-49200 to the container.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Port 5349 (turns:) uses a self-signed certificate; replace cert and pkey with a trusted one if clients use TLS.</div>
</div>
