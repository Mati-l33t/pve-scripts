## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/inngest.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The dashboard on port 8288 has no login: anyone who can reach it can read event data and cancel or rerun functions. Keep it on a trusted network or put an authenticating proxy in front.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Connect your apps with INNGEST_DEV=0, INNGEST_BASE_URL=http://[IP]:8288 and the INNGEST_EVENT_KEY and INNGEST_SIGNING_KEY from /opt/inngest_data/.env.</div>
</div>
