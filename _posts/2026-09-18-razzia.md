## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/razzia.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Host quizzes at http://[IP]:3000/manager with the generated password stored as MANAGER_PASSWORD in /opt/razzia/.env.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Behind a reverse proxy, forward the WebSocket upgrade headers and allow 100 MB uploads, or players stay stuck on connecting.</div>
</div>
