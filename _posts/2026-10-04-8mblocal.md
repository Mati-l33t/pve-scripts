**Default install:**
```bash

```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The UI is behind HTTP Basic auth: user admin, password AUTH_PASS in /opt/8mblocal_data/.env. Change it under Settings &gt; Authentication.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Uploads and results are deleted after 1 hour. Download results right away or raise the retention under Settings.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Hardware encoders are only offered when a test encode passes at startup. For NVENC, the NVIDIA libraries in the container must match the host driver version.</div>
</div>
