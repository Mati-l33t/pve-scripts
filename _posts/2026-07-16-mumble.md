## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVE/main/ct/mumble.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The generated SuperUser password is displayed once during installation. Reset it later with: mumble-server -ini /etc/mumble/mumble-server.ini -supw <password></div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Allow both TCP and UDP traffic on port 64738 through any firewall or router.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The Ice administration endpoint is restricted to localhost and protected with a generated secret.</div>
</div>
