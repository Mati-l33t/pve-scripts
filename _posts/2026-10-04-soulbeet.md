## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Anyone who reaches port 9765 can sign up and take over the instance, including the stored slskd API key. Keep it on a trusted network and change the admin password in Settings &gt; Users right away.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Needs a separate slskd 0.26+ instance. Enter its URL and API key in Settings &gt; Config and mount its downloads folder here, e.g. pct set [CTID] -mp0 /path/to/downloads,mp=/opt/soulbeet_data/downloads.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Mount the music share Navidrome reads at /opt/soulbeet_data/music and add it in Settings &gt; Library. For Navidrome logins and playlists, uncomment NAVIDROME_URL in /opt/soulbeet_data/.env and restart soulbeet.</div>
</div>
