## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>OpenSEO runs with AUTH_MODE=local_noauth: there is no login, anyone who reaches port 3001 is the admin. Keep it on a private network or behind an auth-protected reverse proxy.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>SEO data comes from the paid DataForSEO API (pay-as-you-go, $50 minimum top-up). DATAFORSEO_API_KEY is the base64 of your DataForSEO login:password.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The configuration is baked in at build time: after editing /opt/openseo/.env (DATAFORSEO_API_KEY, ALLOWED_HOST, OPENSEO_TELEMETRY_DISABLED...), a restart is not enough. Rebuild with 'cd /opt/openseo &amp;&amp; env -i PATH="$PATH" HOME=/root pnpm run build &amp;&amp; systemctl restart openseo'.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Behind a reverse proxy, set ALLOWED_HOST to the public hostname in /opt/openseo/.env, then rebuild.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>OpenSEO sends an anonymous usage heartbeat. Set OPENSEO_TELEMETRY_DISABLED=1 in /opt/openseo/.env, then rebuild, to turn it off.</div>
</div>
