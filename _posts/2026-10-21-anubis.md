## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Anubis forwards to TARGET in /etc/anubis/main.env. Set it to the service you want to protect, run systemctl restart anubis@main and point your reverse proxy or DNS at port 8923.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Behind a TLS reverse proxy, set COOKIE_SECURE=true and remove USE_REMOTE_ADDRESS in main.env so client IPs come from X-Forwarded-For.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>One instance protects one TARGET. For another service, copy main.env to /etc/anubis/&lt;name&gt;.env with its own BIND, METRICS_BIND and TARGET, then run systemctl enable --now anubis@&lt;name&gt;.</div>
</div>
