## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>After the first login at http://[IP]/admin/, replace the placeholder email address admin@localhost.local in your profile.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Typecho builds every link from the site address http://&lt;container IP&gt;. Change it under Settings &gt; Basic when the IP changes or the blog sits behind a reverse proxy or domain.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>After an update, open /admin/ and confirm the database upgrade Typecho asks for.</div>
</div>
