## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/langflow.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Sign in with LANGFLOW_SUPERUSER and LANGFLOW_SUPERUSER_PASSWORD from /opt/langflow_data/.env.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Model servers, vector stores and databases on your LAN are blocked until you add their host or subnet (e.g. 192.168.1.0/24) to LANGFLOW_SSRF_ALLOWED_HOSTS in /opt/langflow_data/.env and restart Langflow.</div>
</div>
