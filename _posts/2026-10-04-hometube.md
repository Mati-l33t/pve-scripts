## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/hometube.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>HomeTube has no login. Anyone who reaches port 8501 can download to your media folder, so keep it on a trusted network or behind a reverse proxy with authentication.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Videos are saved to /opt/hometube_data/videos. Bind-mount your Plex, Jellyfin or Emby library there to download straight into it.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>YouTube often needs cookies, even for public videos. Export a cookies.txt from your browser to /opt/hometube_data/cookies/youtube_cookies.txt and reload the page.</div>
</div>
