## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The default login is publicly known. Sign in right away and set your own login, email and password under Account settings &gt; Login and email.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Without SMTP only the default account can sign in. Add the SMTP_* settings (and ALLOW_PUBLIC_REGISTRATION=false for invite-only) to /opt/taskview_data/.env, then restart taskview.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Behind a hostname or reverse proxy, or after an IP change, update APP_URL and CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS in /opt/taskview_data/.env and restart taskview.</div>
</div>
