## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/vert.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Open https://[IP] and accept the self-signed certificate; the converters only work over HTTPS.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Videos the browser cannot convert go to VERT's public vertd servers by default. Set your own vertd instance in the settings to keep them local.</div>
</div>
