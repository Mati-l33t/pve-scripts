## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Open https://[IP] and accept the self-signed certificate, then register the first account. HTTPS is served by nginx (self-signed) because the frontend and passkey/WebAuthn login require a secure browser context.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Passkey/WebAuthn registration does not work against a bare IP address (WebAuthn requires a real domain). Use email/password login on the IP, or put Securo behind a real domain with valid TLS to enable passkeys.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Application secrets and the database URL live in /opt/securo_data/.env, kept outside the app directory so they survive updates. Optional integrations (Pluggy, Enable Banking, SimpleFIN, OIDC, AI agents) can be added there; restart with 'systemctl restart securo securo-worker securo-beat' after changes.</div>
</div>
