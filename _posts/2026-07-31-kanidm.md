## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/deferred/kanidm.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Kanidm needs a domain name, not a bare IP. Open https://kanidm.[IP].nip.io:8443 and accept the self-signed certificate.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>To use your own domain, or if your DNS blocks nip.io, set domain and origin in /etc/kanidm/server.toml before creating accounts. Changing them later needs a domain rename.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Create the admin passwords inside the container with kanidmd recover-account idm_admin (signs in to the web portal) and kanidmd recover-account admin.</div>
</div>
