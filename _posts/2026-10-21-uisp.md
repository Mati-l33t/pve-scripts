## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash

```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>On first access UISP asks you to accept the terms and create the main account (username, password and email). There are no default credentials.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Exposed ports: 443 (web), 80/81, 8089 and 2055/udp (NetFlow).</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Device firmware is managed from Settings &gt; Updates. UISP itself shows a notification when a new version is available and can be updated from the web interface.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Back up from Settings &gt; Backup before updating.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The CRM module may take a few minutes to become available after installation.</div>
</div>
