## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/windmill.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Log in with the default credentials right away: Windmill then asks for a new superadmin account. Until then, anyone who reaches port 8000 can log in.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Webhook URLs and OAuth redirects are built from BASE_URL in /opt/windmill_data/.env. Update it after an IP change or behind a reverse proxy or domain, then restart windmill.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Python, TypeScript on Bun, Bash, DuckDB and SQL work out of the box. Other languages need their runtime installed in the container first.</div>
</div>
