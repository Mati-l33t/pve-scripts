```bash

```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Open https://[IP]/auth/register, accept the self-signed certificate (sign-in only works over HTTPS) and create the first organizer. Sign-up stays open until you set APP_DISABLE_REGISTRATION=true in /opt/hi-events_data/.env.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Without SMTP presets, emails only go to the log in /opt/hi-events_data/storage/logs. For a domain or new IP, change the URLs in /opt/hi-events_data/.env and frontend.env, then restart the hi-events services.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Hi.Events is AGPL-3.0 with an added term: the "Powered by Hi.Events" footer must stay visible unless you buy a licence.</div>
</div>
