## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVE/main/ct/blinko.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>There is no default account: the first sign-up becomes the administrator and closes sign-up. Anyone who reaches the port can claim it, so create it right after the install.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>AI search, chat and tagging stay off until you add a model provider under Settings &gt; AI.</div>
</div>
