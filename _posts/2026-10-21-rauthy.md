## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Accept the self-signed certificate and sign in as admin@localhost with the password_plain value under [bootstrap] in /opt/rauthy_data/config.toml. Change it right away.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Passkeys need a domain, so admin MFA is off. For production, set pub_url, rp_id and rp_origin to your domain before anyone registers a passkey, then add the admin's passkey and set admin_force_mfa = true.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Back up /opt/rauthy_data/config.toml together with the database in /opt/rauthy_data/hiqlite. Without its encryption keys the database is unusable.</div>
</div>
