## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/floppy.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Sign in with DJANGO_SUPERUSER_USERNAME and DJANGO_SUPERUSER_PASSWORD from /opt/floppy_data/.env.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Registration is closed. To let others sign up, set REGISTRATION=True in /opt/floppy_data/.env and run systemctl restart floppy.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Movie, TV and anime search work out of the box. Game search needs your own IGDB (Twitch) client ID and secret under Settings &gt; Metadata.</div>
</div>
