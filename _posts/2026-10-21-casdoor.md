## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The default admin login (organization built-in) is public and controls every user and application. Change the password right away under the user menu &gt; My Account.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>origin in /opt/casdoor_data/conf/app.conf is the OIDC issuer. Behind a reverse proxy or domain, change it before connecting applications and run systemctl restart casdoor.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Casdoor also serves LDAP on port 389 and RADIUS on UDP port 1812. The RADIUS secret is radiusSecret in /opt/casdoor_data/conf/app.conf.</div>
</div>
