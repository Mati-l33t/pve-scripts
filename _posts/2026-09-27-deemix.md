## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/deemix.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Downloading needs your Deezer ARL: copy the arl cookie from deezer.com in your browser's developer tools and paste it under Settings &gt; Login &gt; Log in with ARL. It expires after about three months.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Deemix has no login and uses the saved ARL for every visitor. Keep port 6595 on a trusted network or behind an authenticating reverse proxy.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Music is saved to /opt/deemix_data/music. For a NAS, bind-mount the share there with pct set [CTID] -mp0; in an unprivileged container the host directory must be writable by UID 100000.</div>
</div>
