## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Downloading requires the ARL cookie of your Deezer account. Log in at deezer.com in a browser, copy the value of the arl cookie from the developer tools (Application or Storage tab), then paste it in Deemix under Settings > Login > Log in with ARL. An ARL expires after about three months.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Deemix has no login of its own, and the saved ARL is used for every visitor. Anyone who can reach port 6595 can download with your Deezer account, so keep it on a trusted network or behind an authenticating reverse proxy.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Music is saved to /opt/deemix_data/music. To store it on a NAS or media share, bind-mount that share onto the same path, e.g. pct set [CTID] -mp0 /mnt/media/music,mp=/opt/deemix_data/music. In an unprivileged container the host directory must be writable by UID 100000.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Settings, the saved ARL and logs live in /opt/deemix_data/config, outside the app directory, so updates keep them.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Deemix downloads through your own Deezer account. Whether that is allowed depends on Deezer's terms of use and the law where you live.</div>
</div>
