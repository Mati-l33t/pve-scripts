## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVED/main/ct/castopod.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Complete the initial setup at http://IP/cp-install, then sign in through http://IP/cp-admin.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Set a public HTTPS domain in /opt/castopod/.env before publishing podcasts or enabling ActivityPub federation.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Podcast media is stored in /opt/castopod/public/media. Include it and the MariaDB database in regular container backups.</div>
</div>
