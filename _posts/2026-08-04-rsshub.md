## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/rsshub.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The instance is open to anyone who can reach it. Before exposing it, uncomment ACCESS_KEY in /opt/rsshub.env, restart rsshub and add ?key=... to your feed URLs, or strangers can use it to scrape other sites.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Routes that need credentials (some social networks) take extra variables in /opt/rsshub.env; see the upstream route documentation.</div>
</div>
