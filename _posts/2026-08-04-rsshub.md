## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVED/main/ct/rsshub.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>The instance is open to anyone who can reach it. If it is exposed beyond your LAN, uncomment ACCESS_KEY in /opt/rsshub.env and append ?key=... to your feed URLs, otherwise strangers can use your server to scrape third-party sites.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Caching goes to the local Redis with a 300 second expiry. Many routes scrape HTML, so a short cache means more requests to the target site and a higher chance of being rate-limited or blocked - raise CACHE_EXPIRE if a route starts failing.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Routes that need credentials (some social networks) are configured through additional environment variables in /opt/rsshub.env - see the route documentation upstream.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The build is a large TypeScript project and takes several minutes; 4 GB RAM is the practical minimum for it to complete.</div>
</div>
