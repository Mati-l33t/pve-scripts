```bash

```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>There is no default account. Register the first user under Sign In with an email address and password.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Anyone who reaches the instance can register. Once your accounts exist, set "allowEmailRegister": false in /opt/hedgedoc_data/config.json and run systemctl restart hedgedoc.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Behind a reverse proxy, set domain to your hostname, urlAddPort to false and protocolUseSSL to true in config.json, then restart hedgedoc. Otherwise the page loads without scripts.</div>
</div>
