## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/notemark.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>There is no default account, and sign-up at /auth/signup is open to anyone. Once your accounts exist, set ENABLE_INTERNAL_SIGNUP=false in /opt/notemark_data/.env and run systemctl restart notemark.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>PUBLIC_URL in /opt/notemark_data/.env must be the address you open Note Mark at. Change it for a new IP or a reverse proxy, then restart notemark. Clipboard copy and the PWA need HTTPS.</div>
</div>
