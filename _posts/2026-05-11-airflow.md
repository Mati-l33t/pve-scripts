## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The initial admin password is randomly generated and stored in /opt/airflow/.env (AIRFLOW_ADMIN_PASSWORD).</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Place your DAG files in /opt/airflow/dags/. The scheduler will pick them up automatically.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>

</div>
