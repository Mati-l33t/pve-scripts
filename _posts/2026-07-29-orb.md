## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/ProxmoxVE/main/ct/orb.sh)"
```

  <span class="res-pill res-cpu">CPU: 1 cores</span>

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The sensor starts unlinked and must be claimed before it appears in your account. Run <code>runuser -u orb -- orb link</code> inside the container to sign into Orb.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>View measurements in the Orb app or at https://app.orb.net, or run <code>runuser -u orb -- orb summary</code> in the container for the latest scores as JSON.</div>
</div>
