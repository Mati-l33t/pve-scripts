## Notes

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Log in at http://[IP]:3000 with admin/admin and change the password immediately.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>By default ntopng only sees the container's own traffic. To analyse the whole network you have to feed it traffic - a mirror/SPAN port on your switch, or a flow exporter (NetFlow/sFlow/IPFIX) from your router. Set the interface with the -i line in /etc/ntopng/ntopng.conf.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>This is the Community Edition. Retention, historical flow drilldown and some alerting features are reserved for the paid Pro/Enterprise editions.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Installed from the official ntop apt repository (packages.ntop.org/apt-stable), which pulls in redis-server and its other dependencies automatically. Updates go through apt.</div>
</div>
