## Installation

**Default install:**
```bash
bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/community-scripts/DevScripts/main/ct/mathesar.sh)"
```

## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The first visit asks you to create the admin account. Its two analytics checkboxes are ticked by default; untick them if you do not want to share usage data.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The bundled PostgreSQL only holds Mathesar's own database. Choose 'Create a new database' for your data, or connect to an existing PostgreSQL 14+ server.</div>
</div>

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>Behind a reverse proxy, set ALLOWED_HOSTS in /opt/mathesar_data/.env to your domain instead of * and forward the Host and X-Forwarded-Proto headers.</div>
</div>
