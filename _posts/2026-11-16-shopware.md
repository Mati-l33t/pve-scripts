## Notes

<div class="info-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-info-circle"></i>
  <div>The admin login is saved to ~/shopware.creds inside the container. The Administration is at /admin.</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Behind an HTTPS reverse proxy (NPM, Traefik, Caddy, Cloudflare Tunnel), run inside the container: shopware-proxy shop.example.com (your public domain). It makes Shopware trust the proxy, sets APP_URL to https and rebuilds the config. Then, in the Administration, add https://shop.example.com to the Storefront sales channel (Sales Channels &gt; Storefront &gt; Domains).</div>
</div>

<div class="warn-callout">
  <i class="fas fa-exclamation-triangle"></i>
  <div>Major Shopware upgrades (e.g. 6.7 to 6.8) are not applied by the update and must follow the official upgrade guide.</div>
</div>
